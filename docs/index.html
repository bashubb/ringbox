How it works

From a sound to an alarm in under two minutes.

1

Record or import

Say something you’d genuinely like to hear, or bring in a sound or song from Files. Up to 1:30 of material.

2

Keep the best part

Drag the edges around the moment that matters — up to 29 seconds, with fades and a volume boost if you want them.

3

Hear it ring for real

Schedule a test alarm two minutes out and hear exactly how your morning will sound.

Rings when it matters

A real system alarm, not a notification.

Ringbox schedules alarms with Apple’s AlarmKit, so they ring like the built-in Clock app — on the Lock Screen, in the Dynamic Island and through Silent Mode and Focus.
