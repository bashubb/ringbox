FAQ

Before your first alarm

Will my alarm ring in Silent Mode or Focus?
Yes. Ringbox schedules real system alarms with Apple’s AlarmKit, which ring on the Lock Screen and through Silent Mode and Focus, like the built in Clock app.
How long can a sound be?
You can record or import up to 1:30 and keep up to 29 seconds of it as the alarm sound, the most iOS allows for an alarm.
Can I use a song?
Yes. Import an audio file you have in Files and keep the best part. Protected songs from streaming services can’t be imported.
Can I test an alarm?
Yes. During setup Ringbox schedules a test alarm about two minutes ahead so you can hear exactly how it sounds.
What if I delete a sound an alarm uses?
Ringbox asks first. Alarms that used it switch to the system sound, so they still ring.
Do my recordings leave my iPhone?
No. Your sounds are stored on your iPhone and are never uploaded. There is no account.
Which iPhones does Ringbox support?
Any iPhone running iOS 26.1 or later, the version that brought AlarmKit.
